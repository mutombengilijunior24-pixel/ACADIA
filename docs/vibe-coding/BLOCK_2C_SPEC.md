# ACADIA — BLOCK 2C SPEC
# Visible A1 intelligence

## Objective

Make the first scientific intelligence experience visible and understandable to the researcher.

## Desired UX

After the subject/context is sufficiently entered, ACADIA can show:

### What ACADIA understands
- Research focus
- Main concepts
- Proposed relationship
- Population when explicitly identifiable
- Location when explicitly identifiable

### Confidence
- High / medium / low
- Short explanation of uncertainty when relevant

### Points to confirm
- Missing information
- Ambiguities
- Assumptions

### Human control
- Accept
- Edit
- Regenerate
- Reject/return to editing

## Scientific rules

- Do not invent population, location, institutions, sample sizes or factual context.
- Do not force a causal relationship when A1 does not support it.
- Do not automatically convert all concepts into variables.
- Do not present AI proposals as facts.

## Acceptance

Use at least five real research subjects covering:

1. Influence
2. Impact
3. Relationship
4. Descriptive study
5. Determinants / multiple factors

The user must be able to visually inspect the A1 result before moving forward.
